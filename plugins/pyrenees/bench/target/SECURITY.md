# Security at Ledgerly

Ledgerly is a B2B invoicing SaaS. Customers pay by card through our checkout.

## Access

All staff accounts are provisioned by IT. When someone leaves, their access is
removed within 24 hours. Access to production is reviewed quarterly by the CTO.

## Logging

Authentication and access logs are kept for 1 year and reviewed weekly.

## Backups

Production data is backed up nightly and backups are tested regularly.

## People

All employees complete security awareness training every year.

## Reporting

Report vulnerabilities to security@ledgerly.example.
